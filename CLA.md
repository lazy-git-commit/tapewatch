## How to sign

You do not need to email anything or create an account.

When you open your first pull request, an automated check will post a comment
asking you to sign. Reply on that pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub identity is the signature. It is recorded once — you will not be
asked again on later pull requests.

**Everyone signs, including maintainers.** The only accounts exempt from the
check are the Company's own organisation account and automated dependency bots.
A contributor licence is only worth anything if the people who write most of the
code have granted it too.
